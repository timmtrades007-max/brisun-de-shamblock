The company

A long drive does not land under a tree. It lands in the middle of a wide hole, in full sun, after a swing that already made you sweat. Shamblock is the coat for that round: Bryson’s variables, aimed at the sun.

Bomb SPF 50Face and neck. On before you swing out of your shoes. The tee-shot coat. Holds through a sweaty speed block.
Carry 30Arms and legs. For the walk to a ball that flew past the shade. The further it carries, the longer you are in the open.
Single Length SPFOne formula, every surface. Same religion as one length of iron. Ears, neck, the back of the hands.
Smash StickThe white zinc stripe. Center-face for the nose. On before the first ball on the range, any index over 3.
80-Minute MistA speed swing soaks a coat. Two passes on the walk, then another driver. Reapply at 80 minutes or at the turn.
Lip 5.5°SPF balm named for a low-loft driver. The pocket club. Stays with the tee, not in the car.

The protocol

  1. Smash Stick on the nose before the first speed swing.
  2. Bomb SPF 50 on the face. Carry 30 on the arms.
  3. Single Length on the ears and neck. The tee box has no roof.
  4. 80-Minute Mist after the session, or at the turn, then hit driver again.
  5. Lip 5.5° lives in the pocket with the tee.
